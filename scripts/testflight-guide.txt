Thank you for testing longbook. Start from nothing and go in this order. When something confuses you, breaks or feels slow, take a screenshot and send it with TestFlight's feedback, or write to contact@longbook.app.

WHAT LONGBOOK IS
A private memory for the money you pay again and again: subscriptions, rent, insurance, loans, fees. It never connects to your bank. Your longbook is a folder in your own iCloud Drive.

1. GET STARTED (3 steps)
- Sign in with Apple (iPhone), Google, or your email (a code is emailed to you).
- Activate your longbook: the one-time purchase costs nothing on TestFlight. Try Restore Purchase and See Demo too.
- Import a statement: tap Securely Upload Statement and pick a bank or card statement (PDF, CSV or Excel), or tap Skip.

2. THE STATEMENT REVIEW
- It lists Commitments, Past expenses, People and Accounts, all ticked.
- Untick anything wrong and tap Add. Proposals then shows what was recorded; keep or undo it there.
- Did it miss a payment, a person or an account the statement shows? Tell us.

3. AN EMPTY LONGBOOK
- After Skip, the Dashboard offers three ways to start: import a statement, add a commitment, or Use My Own AI.
- On the Mac, drop a statement file onto the Dashboard.

4. EVERYDAY USE
- The + button: add a commitment (name, how often, amount) or a one-time expense.
- Open a commitment: edit it, mark it paid, bookmark it, cancel it. Delete a planned charge and choose this one or every one after.
- Calendar, Charges and Reports (one for each month with charges).
- People and Accounts: star, search and filter.
- Each screen shows a short tutorial once. Replay them in Settings > Tutorials.

5. SUPPORT
- The Support button sits in the corner of every screen. Ask a question, attach a screenshot with the camera button, or email the team.

6. YOUR AI
- Settings > AI Connections: Let AI Read My longbook, then connect Claude or ChatGPT. Use My Own AI copies the prompt for you.
- Ask it "What do I pay next?" or "Add my statement".

7. SETTINGS AND PRIVACY
- Optimize your data: the people and accounts that help longbook read statements.
- Account: your licence, longbook Cloud, Request a Refund, Sign Out, Delete Account.
- Data & privacy: what leaves your device, and opening a longbook from another folder.
- On the Mac: open at login, the next payment in the menu bar, hiding the Dock icon.

8. ALSO TRY

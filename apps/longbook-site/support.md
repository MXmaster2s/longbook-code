# Support

> Ask longbook Support: answers from the help center, or the maker of longbook when you ask for a person.

- Page: https://longbook.app/support/

Ask a question and get an answer from the longbook help center. If it can't help, tap **Talk to a person** and the maker of longbook answers you. You can attach a screenshot.

[Ask Support](https://longbook.app/support/)

- **Help center**: [longbook.chat.sh](https://longbook.chat.sh) — every guide, searchable.
- **Email**: [contact@longbook.app](mailto:contact@longbook.app)

Please don't send account numbers, card details or amounts. We never ask for the contents of your longbook. Support runs on chat.sh and loads only when you open it; the [privacy policy](https://longbook.app/privacy-policy/#collect) says what it keeps.

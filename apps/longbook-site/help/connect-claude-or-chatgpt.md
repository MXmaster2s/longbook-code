# Connect Claude or ChatGPT

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/connect-claude-or-chatgpt/

Your AI can read your longbook and add to it: bills it finds in your email, subscriptions, receipts, anything you point it at.

## On a Mac, with Claude

In **AI Connections** (on Mac, the **AI** pane of the Settings window), tap **Set Up Claude Desktop**. Claude Desktop then reads your longbook folder directly, with no copy on our server.

## From any device, with Claude or ChatGPT

1. In Claude or ChatGPT, add a custom connector with this address: `https://longbook.app/mcp`
2. Sign in with your longbook account when asked.

First, in **AI Connections**, turn on **Let AI Read My longbook**. This uses longbook Cloud, because your AI reaches a copy of your longbook kept on our server. Turning it off deletes the copy at once. Your purchase includes 12 months of Cloud.

To let your AI add things, also turn on **Allow proposals**.

## Set it up with a prompt

At setup's statement step, **Use My Own AI** shows three steps:

1. **Enable** lets your AI read your longbook.
2. **Claude** or **ChatGPT** copies a prompt that adds longbook to that AI. In Claude it installs the longbook plugin, or adds the connector where plugins can't be installed. In ChatGPT it creates a plugin named longbook, with its website and icon. Paste it into your AI.
3. **Copy Prompt** copies the words that ask your AI to finish setting up your longbook.

## What your AI can do

Try asking:

- "Find subscriptions from my email"
- "Add my statement"
- "What do I pay next?"
- "What is coming in the next 30 days?"

Every change your AI makes waits on the **Proposals** screen with its name on it. Keep it or undo it with one tap. longbook also keeps your past versions, so you can go back.

## If it doesn't connect

- Check that you're signed in to the same longbook account in the app and in your AI.
- Remove the connector and add it again.
- Still stuck? In Support, tap **Ask a person** to open an email to the team, and tell us which AI you use and the message it shows.

Still have a question? Tap **Support** in the corner of any screen in the app, or write to contact@longbook.app and a person answers.

# Refunds, restoring a purchase and Family Sharing

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/refunds-restoring-a-purchase-and-family-sharing/

## Get a refund

Within 7 days of buying longbook, open the **Account** screen and tap **Request a Refund**. Apple handles the refund under its own terms. If the button doesn't show, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A refund also ends the 12 months of longbook Cloud that came with the purchase, for you and for your family.

Refunds of Cloud's monthly fee are handled by Apple too. Cancelling Cloud stops the next renewal. It doesn't refund the current month.

## Restore your purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the **Account** screen and tap **Restore Purchase**. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

## Family Sharing

Your purchase covers everyone in your Apple family:

- Each person installs longbook on their own devices and uses their own longbook account.
- Each family member also gets the free 12 months of Cloud.
- Each person's longbook is their own folder in their own iCloud. Family members don't see each other's records unless you share a report.

If a family member doesn't see longbook as purchased, check that purchase sharing is on in your Apple family settings, then restore on their device.

## Charged twice?

In Support, tap **Ask a person** to open an email to the team, and tell us the date of each charge. Please don't send card numbers. Apple's receipt email is enough.

Still have a question? Open **Support** in the app, under **Settings**, then **Help & about**, or write to contact@longbook.app and a person answers.

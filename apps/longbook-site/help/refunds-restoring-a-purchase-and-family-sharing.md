# Refunds, restoring a purchase and Family Sharing

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/refunds-restoring-a-purchase-and-family-sharing/

## Request a refund

Refunds of App Store purchases are made by Apple, and Apple decides each request. Within 7 days of buying longbook, open the **Account** screen and tap **Request a Refund** to send your request to Apple. You can also request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, which is also the place to go if the button doesn't show.

If Apple refunds the purchase, the licence ends, and so do the 12 months of longbook Cloud that came with the purchase, for you and for your family.

If you bought in longbook for Mac downloaded from longbook.app, write to Support or contact@longbook.app within 7 days. We refund you through Polar, to the payment method you paid with, and the licence and its 12 months of Cloud end. Receipts are in Polar's customer portal at polar.sh/ccs/portal.

Refunds of Cloud's monthly fee are requested from Apple the same way, and Apple decides them too. Cancelling Cloud stops the next renewal. It doesn't refund the current month.

## Restore your purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the **Account** screen and tap **Restore Purchase**. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Apple may ask you to sign in to your Apple Account, the one you use for the App Store. That sheet is Apple's, not longbook's: a longbook account signed in with Google or Apple has no password, and none is asked for here.

When the purchase is found, the screen says **Purchase restored**. When none is on that Apple Account, it says so, and you can sign out of the App Store and in with the account you bought with.

## Family Sharing

Family Sharing applies to a licence bought in the App Store. An App Store purchase covers everyone in your Apple family:

- Each person installs longbook on their own devices and signs in with their own longbook account. longbook unlocks on its own once they sign in.
- Each family member also gets the free 12 months of Cloud.
- Each person's longbook is their own folder in their own iCloud. Family members don't see each other's records unless you share a report.

A licence bought in longbook for Mac from longbook.app is not shared through Apple.

If a family member doesn't see longbook as purchased, check that purchase sharing is on in your Apple family settings. **Restore Purchase** on the **Account** screen is there if it still doesn't unlock.

A family member's access lasts while the purchase is shared with them. It ends if the organiser stops sharing purchases or they leave the family.

## Charged twice?

In Support, tap **Ask a person** to open an email to the team, and tell us the date of each charge. Please don't send card numbers. Apple's receipt email is enough.

Still have a question? Tap **Support** in the corner of any screen in the app, or write to contact@longbook.app and a person answers.

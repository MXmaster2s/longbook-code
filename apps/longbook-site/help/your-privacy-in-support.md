# Your privacy in Support

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/your-privacy-in-support/

## We never see inside your longbook

Your records live in your own iCloud. Support never asks for them, and we can't open them.

## What to leave out of a message

Please don't paste or attach:

- account or card numbers, card details or PINs
- amounts, balances or statements
- passwords or sign-in codes

To show a problem, describe it, or attach a screenshot with the camera button. The screenshot goes with your message to the AI, which reads it to see your screen and never repeats what it shows. It may show amounts, so check it first.

## Who answers

longbook Support is an AI. It answers from these help articles, using Anthropic's Claude through longbook's server. When it doesn't know, or you'd rather ask a person, tap **Ask a person**: it opens your own email to contact@longbook.app with the conversation in it, and you send it yourself. Someone on the team replies to you by email.

## What Support keeps

- **On your device:** your conversation, so you can come back to it.
- **On our server:** nothing you wrote. We only count questions, note which articles answered them and how you rated them, and, when an article was missing, a short topic with no names or numbers, so we know what to write next.
- **In our inbox:** only an email you choose to send us.
- **At Anthropic:** your messages are used only to answer you, kept briefly under its commercial terms, and never used to train its models.

A screenshot you attach goes with that one message, to our server and to Anthropic, the same way as your words. Our server doesn't keep it. From the attachment you can send it to the team by email instead.

## Forget your conversation

In the app, tap **Clear support history** on the Help & about screen. To have an email deleted from our inbox, write to contact@longbook.app.

Still have a question? Tap **Support** in the corner of any screen in the app, or write to contact@longbook.app and a person answers.

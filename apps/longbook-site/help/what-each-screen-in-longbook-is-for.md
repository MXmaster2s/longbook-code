# What each screen in longbook is for

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/what-each-screen-in-longbook-is-for/

## On iPhone

The bottom bar has five places:

- **Dashboard**: the next payment, the month's spending and where it went.
- **Calendar**: every payment on its day.
- **Add**: a new commitment or expense.
- **Reports**: one report for every month that has a confirmed charge.
- **Settings**: everything else.

Settings opens everything else: **Proposals**, **Insights**, **Bookmarks**, **Accounts**, **People**, **Import a statement** and **Add accounts and people found in statements**, **Trips**, **AI Connections**, **Display & currency**, **Data & privacy**, **Optimize your data**, **Try the demo**, **Tips**, **Tutorials**, **Help & about**, **What’s new**, and your account at the top.

## On Mac

The sidebar lists them all:

- **Overview**: Dashboard, Reports, Calendar, Commitments, Insights, Proposals.
- **Payments**: Accounts, and under it your starred accounts.
- **People**: People, and under it your starred people.
- At the bottom: **Add commitment**, **Settings** and the bookmark icon. **Settings** opens a menu with **Settings…** (⌘,), **What’s New**, **Send Feedback…**, **Check for Updates…** and **Quit longbook**.

**Payments** and **People** fold away with a click on their name. Star an account or a person on its screen to keep it in the sidebar.

## What each one is

- **Commitments**: every payment ahead, grouped by month. **Past** lists every charge made.
- **Proposals**: what your AI, Siri or a statement import wrote, to keep or undo.
- **Insights**: short notes your AI left on your cards.
- **Accounts**: your banks, cards and wallets, each with its balance.
- **People**: who each payment is for.
- **Bookmarks**: the commitments you check most.

## Settings

On Mac, Settings is a window of its own, like the Mac's System Settings. Its sidebar has a search field, your longbook account, then **General**, **Appearance**, **AI**, **Statements**, **Data & Privacy** and **Tutorials**. Back and Forward (⌘[ and ⌘]) move between pages. **AI** is where you connect your AI, copy prompts, and set up Siri and Shortcuts; on iPhone that's **AI Connections**.

## Questions

### Where do I add a payment?

Tap **Add** on iPhone, or **Add commitment** in the Mac sidebar.

### Where do I see what my AI changed?

In **Proposals**.

Still have a question? Tap **Support** in the corner of any screen in the app, or write to contact@longbook.app and a person answers.

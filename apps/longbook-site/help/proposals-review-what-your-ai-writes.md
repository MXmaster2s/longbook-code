# Proposals: review what your AI writes

- Page: https://longbook.app/help/proposals-review-what-your-ai-writes/

Every row that an AI, Siri, a statement import or another program writes into longbook shows in **Proposals**, with its writer's name, to keep or discard.

## How it works

- Rows are listed in a tab for each kind: charges, commitments, accounts, people and categories.
- The search finds a row by name. **Filter** shows only new, edited or removed rows.
- A changed row shows each field before and after.
- **Keep All** accepts the proposal. **Discard All** writes back the state from before it. On Mac both stand beside the title.
- Before you decide, you can edit or delete a row that was added.

## Not a queue

A proposal is already in your longbook when you see it, and the rest of the app counts it. **Keep All** confirms it. **Discard All** removes it, including anything edited since.

## History

**Show History**, at the foot of the list, opens the last 50 versions of your longbook, each with its writer, time and what it changed. **Restore** writes an earlier version back as one more proposal. It deletes nothing.

## Questions

### Can I keep one row and discard another?

No. **Keep All** and **Discard All** answer the whole proposal. Delete or edit a row first if you need to.

### What counts as a proposal?

Writes from an AI through the MCP server, Siri, the Shortcuts action, or another program editing the file. Edits you make in the app's own forms and statement imports don't wait for review; an import can be taken back with **Undo Import**, or from **History**. Notes your AI leaves on cards don't show here.

### Can my AI change things without my knowing?

No. Every proposal names its writer. Through the online connection, a signed-in AI can only make proposals, and only after **Allow proposals** is on.

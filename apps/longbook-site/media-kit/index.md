## About longbook

The same description at three lengths, written to be pasted as it is.

### One line

longbook remembers your financial commitments, reminds you before each one is due, and answers all of it to your AI.

### Short

longbook remembers and reminds you of your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, loans, rent, school fees, anything that costs money and comes back. It is a native app for Mac and iPhone, keeps everything in your own iCloud, and answers all of it to your AI.

### Long

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments. It keeps every subscription, insurance premium, loan, rent payment and renewal (the amount, how often it repeats and when it is due next) and reminds you before each one comes round, on iPhone and Mac. Your AI can fill it in: through longbook’s MCP server, Claude or ChatGPT reads the receipts in your email and writes them in, and every change waits in the app for you to keep or undo. Everything lives in a plain file in your own iCloud. longbook is a one-time purchase, $38 for the first 100 buyers, with a 7-day refund.

The name is one word, all lowercase, even at the start of a sentence. Write **longbook**, not Longbook, LongBook or Long Book.

## Facts

- **Name:** longbook
- **What it is:** A memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, loans, rent, school fees and anything else that costs money and comes back.
- **Platforms:** Mac (macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon) and iPhone (iOS 17 or later). Native apps on both.
- **Availability:** Beta through TestFlight on Mac and iPhone. The App Store release is coming soon.
- **Current version:** 1.3.3
- **Price:** A lifetime license, paid once: $38 for the first 100 buyers, then $98 for the next 250, then $149. It covers every longbook app, on Mac and iPhone now and on iPad, Apple Watch, iPhone Duo and Android as they arrive. A refund can be asked for within 7 days, and 12 months of longbook Cloud are included, for the buyer's Apple family too.
- **Where the data lives:** In a plain JSON file in the person’s own iCloud Drive. It is copied to longbook’s server only if they switch that on.
- **Works with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Codex CLI and Gemini CLI, through longbook’s MCP server at longbook.app/mcp.
- **Founder:** Rohit Kundliwal
- **Website:** longbook.app
- **Press contact:** contact@longbook.app

### What it does

- **Remembers every commitment.** The amount, how often it repeats and when it is due next, for every subscription, premium, loan and renewal.
- **Lets your AI fill it in.** Connected over MCP, Claude or ChatGPT reads the receipts and renewal notices in your email and writes them into longbook.
- **Keeps every change reviewable.** Every edit an AI makes waits in the Proposals section with its author recorded, ready to keep or undo. Earlier versions can be restored.
- **Shows what is next at a glance.** An iPhone widget and a Mac menu bar item show the next payment without opening the app.
- **Turns a month into a report.** A month of spending goes to family as a link that expires, and their comments come back into longbook.

## Founder

**Rohit Kundliwal**, founder of longbook. On X as @rohthebuilder.

> I built longbook for myself. I wanted my commitments remembered properly, and I wanted my AI to go through my email and fill them in, so that what is coming shows up on my iPhone and my MacBook without me chasing it.
>
> I also wanted to know what a month actually costs, and to be able to hand that to my wife rather than explain it. So the reports are in the app too. Every part of it started as something I needed — it is the thing I stopped forgetting.

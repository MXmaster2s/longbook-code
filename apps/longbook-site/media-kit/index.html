About longbook

The same description at three lengths, written to be pasted as it is.

longbook remembers your financial commitments, reminds you before each one is due, and answers all of it to your AI.

longbook remembers and reminds you of your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, loans, rent, school fees, anything that costs money and comes back. It is a native app for Mac and iPhone, keeps everything in your own iCloud, and answers all of it to your AI.

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments. It keeps every subscription, insurance premium, loan, rent payment and renewal (the amount, how often it repeats and when it is due next) and reminds you before each one comes round, on iPhone and Mac. Your AI can fill it in: through longbook’s MCP server, Claude or ChatGPT reads the receipts in your email and writes them in, and every change waits in the app for you to keep or undo. Everything lives in a plain file in your own iCloud. longbook is a one-time purchase, $38 for the first 100 buyers, with a 7-day refund.

The name is one word, all lowercase, even at the start of a sentence.

What it does

Remembers every commitment

The amount, how often it repeats and when it is due next, for every subscription, premium, loan and renewal.

Lets your AI fill it in

Connected over MCP, Claude or ChatGPT reads the receipts and renewal notices in your email and writes them into longbook.

Keeps every change reviewable

Every edit an AI makes waits in the Changes section with its author recorded, ready to keep or undo. Earlier versions can be restored.

Shows what is next at a glance

An iPhone widget and a Mac menu bar item show the next payment without opening the app.

Turns a month into a report

A month of spending goes to family as a link that expires, and their comments come back into longbook.

Logos

SVG for anything that scales, PNG for everything else. The logo is the book with the wordmark beside it; the app icon and the book also stand on their own.

Using the logo

  • Leave space around the logo at least as wide as the mark.
  • Below 24 px tall, use the one-colour mark.
  • Keep the colour logo off busy photos and off violet; the one-colour mark works there.
  • Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the mark or the wordmark.

Founder

Photo, JPGSquare, JPG

Rohit Kundliwal

Founder of longbook

“I built longbook for myself. I wanted my commitments remembered properly, and I wanted my AI to go through my email and fill them in, so that what is coming shows up on my iPhone and my MacBook without me chasing it.

I also wanted to know what a month actually costs, and to be able to hand that to my wife rather than explain it. So the reports are in the app too. Every part of it started as something I needed — it is the thing I stopped forgetting.”

## Join the 1.5.0 TestFlight

longbook is a private memory for the money you pay again and again: subscriptions, rent, insurance, loans and fees. It never connects to your bank, and your longbook is a folder in your own iCloud Drive. Version 1.5.0 changes how you start, so we would like people who have never seen longbook to try it from nothing.

1. Install Apple's TestFlight app on your iPhone or Mac, if you do not have it.
2. Open the [longbook TestFlight link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/g66Nd3Na) and tap Accept, then Install.
3. Open longbook and follow the steps below.

TestFlight is Apple's way of trying an app before it is released. Nothing is charged on TestFlight.

## What is new in 1.5.0

These are the changes you will notice in the first ten minutes.

- **Getting started takes three steps.** Welcome and sign in, activate your longbook, then import a statement. Once a statement is kept, your longbook opens.
- **A review you can read.** After you import a statement, one list shows Commitments, Past expenses, People and Accounts, in that order, with every item ticked. You untick what is wrong and tap Add.
- **Every person and account the statement prints.** The review offers each one, your own other accounts included, so you untick rather than miss one.
- **An empty longbook has a place to start.** If you skip the statement, the Dashboard offers three ways in: import a statement, add a commitment by hand, or use your own AI. On the Mac, you can also drop a statement file on it.
- **Support on every screen.** A Support button sits in the corner of iPhone and Mac. You can ask a question, attach a screenshot with the camera button, or email the team.
- **Delete this charge, or every one after it.** Deleting a planned charge now asks whether to remove only that one, or that one and all the ones after it.
- **Use My Own AI.** In Settings, this copies a prompt that sets longbook up in Claude or ChatGPT, so you do not have to write it.
- **A tutorial for each screen.** The first time you open a screen, a short tutorial shows what it is for. You can replay any of them in Settings, then Tutorials.

## What to test

Start from nothing and go in this order. Each step says what we most want to know.

### 1. Get started

Sign in with Apple on iPhone, with Google, or with your email, where a code is sent to you. Then activate your longbook. Try Restore Purchase and See Demo as well.

Then tap Securely Upload Statement and pick a bank or card statement, as a PDF, CSV or Excel file, or tap Skip. Which part made you pause?

### 2. The statement review

The list shows Commitments, Past expenses, People and Accounts, all ticked. Untick anything wrong and tap Add. Proposals then shows what was recorded, and you can keep or undo it there.

Did it miss a payment, a person or an account that your statement shows? Tell us which one, and which bank it came from. Your statement is read by an AI and is covered by the [privacy policy](https://longbook.app/privacy-policy/), which longbook asks you to accept before the first one.

### 3. An empty longbook

If you tapped Skip, look at the Dashboard. Can you tell what to do next without reading anything? On the Mac, drop a statement file onto it.

### 4. Everyday use

Tap the + button and add a commitment, with its name, how often it repeats and its amount, or a one-time expense. Open a commitment to edit it, mark it paid, bookmark it or cancel it. Delete a planned charge and choose this one or every one after it.

Look through Calendar, Charges and Reports, which has one report for each month with charges. Open People and Accounts, star one, and try the search and the filter.

### 5. Support

Tap the Support button in the corner of any screen. Ask a question, attach a screenshot with the camera button, or email the team. A screenshot can show amounts, so look at it before you send it.

### 6. Your AI

In Settings, open AI Connections, choose Let AI Read My longbook, and connect Claude or ChatGPT. Use My Own AI copies the prompt for you. Then ask it "What do I pay next?" or "Add my statement". How far did you get before you got stuck?

### 7. Settings and privacy

Open Optimize your data, where you can add the people and accounts that help longbook read statements. In Account you will find your licence, longbook Cloud, Request a Refund, Sign Out and Delete Account. Data & privacy shows what leaves your device, and lets you open a longbook from another folder.

On the Mac, try opening at login, the next payment in the menu bar, and hiding the Dock icon.

### 8. Also try

Dark mode and larger text. The Home Screen widget on iPhone and the menu bar item on the Mac. The same account on a second device.

## How to send feedback

When something confuses you, breaks or feels slow, take a screenshot. Send it with TestFlight's feedback, or write to [contact@longbook.app](mailto:contact@longbook.app). A line about what you expected to happen helps more than a long report.

## About longbook

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, rent, loans and every other payment that comes back. It runs on iPhone and Mac and keeps its records as plain files in your own iCloud.

The next payment stays in view on the iPhone widget and in the Mac menu bar. Your AI can read your longbook and update it through longbook's MCP server, and every change it makes shows in Proposals, to keep or undo.

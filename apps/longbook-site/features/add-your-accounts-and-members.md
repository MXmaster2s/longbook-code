# Add your accounts and members

> People are who your commitments are for. Accounts are what they are paid from. Each commitment can name one of each, and Commitments filters by both.

- Page: https://longbook.app/features/add-your-accounts-and-members/
- Group: Track
- Platforms: iPhone and Mac

## On the screen

**People** on iPhone and Mac: one row for each person, with their relationship, such as Family, Household help or Landlord, and how many commitments name them. A tap on iPhone opens their card; on Mac a table sorts by any column, with the chosen person's card beside it. The **Unassigned** row holds the commitments that name no one.

A star keeps a person or an account at the top, and on Mac the sidebar lists only the starred ones. A search field and a filter, **Relationship** for people and **Type** for accounts, narrow either list.

## What it does

### Who a payment is for

A person is anyone a commitment is for: family, household help, a landlord, a tenant or a vendor. An optional relationship gives their role, from longbook's list or typed in, such as Cook or Self.

### What pays it

An account is what a commitment is paid from: a bank account, a credit or debit card, UPI, a wallet or cash. Each account has one **Owner**, a person, or **Shared** for a joint account.

### One card per person

Each card counts a person's commitments, charges and accounts and names the next one due. **Add Commitment** opens the form with that person chosen; **Show All** lists every commitment for them.

## Why it exists

A household pays for more than one person, from more than one account. A cook's salary, a parent's insurance and a child's school fees each belong to someone. Naming the person and the account shows who each payment is for and where it is paid from.

## Questions

### What happens to commitments when a person is deleted?

They stay, without a person. Before the delete, longbook states how many commitments, one-time charges and accounts name that person. Their accounts stay too, and belong to no one in particular. Deleting an account leaves its commitments in place the same way.

### Can a bank statement add people and accounts?

Yes. With **Add accounts and people found in statements** on, which it is unless you turn it off, an import adds the statement's own account, the people its lines name, and the accounts and people that transfers and card payments go to. Each one is shown in the review first, matched to what you already have. With the switch off they start unticked. More on [Import your bank statements](https://longbook.app/features/import-your-bank-statements.md).

### Are the people and accounts from setup added to these lists?

No. The answers you give in setup are kept as hints for reading statements. People and Accounts list only what you add and what statements show.

### Does a person need a longbook account?

No. A person is a name in your records, not a sign-in. To show someone a month, [share a report](https://longbook.app/features/share-monthly-expense-reports-with-others.md) as a link.

## About longbook

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, rent, loans and every other payment that comes back. It runs on iPhone and Mac and keeps its records as plain files in your own iCloud.

The next payment stays in view on the iPhone widget and in the Mac menu bar. Your AI can read your longbook and update it through longbook's MCP server, and every change it makes shows in Proposals, to keep or undo.

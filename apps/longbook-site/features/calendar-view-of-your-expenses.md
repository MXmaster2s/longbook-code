# Calendar view of your expenses

> Every payment on its day in a month calendar, twelve months back and twelve ahead, with each day's list one tap away.

- Page: https://longbook.app/features/calendar-view-of-your-expenses/
- Group: Understand
- Platforms: iPhone and Mac

## On the screen

The **Calendar** on iPhone and Mac for September 2026: each day with a payment shows category dots and a count, and on iPhone a transfer on the 24th shows its own mark. The selected day's details sit below the grid on iPhone and beside it on Mac.

## What it does

### Every payment on its day

Each day shows how many payments fall on it, with up to three dots in the colours of their categories. A transfer, such as a card bill, carries its own mark and stays out of the count.

### Twelve months either way

The calendar steps a month at a time, twelve months back and twelve ahead, with the arrows or a sideways swipe. **Today** returns to the current month, and past days are dimmed.

### The day in detail

A tap on a day lists its payments and transfers with their amounts. From today on, each has **Mark Paid**, and a day with everything paid shows a check mark. **Show in Commitments** opens the full list, narrowed to that day.

## Why it exists

Payments are easier to plan around on the days they fall. A month grid shows the busy days and the empty ones together, drawn from the same records as the list and the dashboard.

## Questions

### Does the calendar show money in?

No. It shows payments and transfers. Money in, such as salary received, is shown on the [dashboard](https://longbook.app/features/get-spending-insights.md), under the month's total expenses.

### Does longbook add payments to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar?

No. The calendar is drawn inside longbook from your records, and nothing is written to another calendar app.

### Does longbook remind me before a payment?

Yes. The next payment stays in view on the [iPhone Home Screen widget](https://longbook.app/features/add-longbook-widget-to-your-iphone.md) and in the [Mac menu bar](https://longbook.app/features/see-your-next-payment-in-the-mac-menu-bar.md).

## About longbook

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, rent, loans and every other payment that comes back. It runs on iPhone and Mac and keeps its records as plain files in your own iCloud.

The next payment stays in view on the iPhone widget and in the Mac menu bar. Your AI can read your longbook and update it through longbook's MCP server, and every change it makes shows in Changes, to keep or undo.

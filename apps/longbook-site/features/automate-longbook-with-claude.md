## What it does

### Answers from your own records

Claude calls longbook's tools to answer: the next payment, what is due in the next two weeks, spending by category, account or member, and a search across every commitment.

### Writes that wait for you

Claude can add commitments and charges, mark a charge paid, cancel a commitment or leave an insight. Each change is checked against longbook's format and shows in **Proposals**, to keep or undo.

### Two ways to connect

On a Mac, **Set Up Claude Desktop** connects Claude to the folder directly, with no hosted copy; what Claude reads goes to Claude as part of the conversation. Anywhere else, Claude connects to `longbook.app/mcp` and reads a hosted copy that only your account can reach.

## Why it exists

The questions people ask about money are rarely the ones an app has a button for. A memory an AI can read answers them in plain words, and one it can write to stays current without typing.

## Questions

### Which AI assistants work with longbook?

Claude, ChatGPT and any other assistant that supports MCP connectors. Claude Desktop on a Mac can also connect to the folder directly. The full setup is on the [Automations page](https://longbook.app/automations/).

### Can Claude change my longbook without me knowing?

No. Every change Claude makes shows in Proposals with Claude named as its author. Through the hosted connector, a signed-in Claude can only suggest changes, and only after **Allow proposals** is turned on in the app.

### What does the hosted copy hold?

A copy of the memory file, uploaded when the MCP server is enabled. It is deleted when the server is switched off, or after ninety days without the app being opened.

## About longbook

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, rent, loans and every other payment that comes back. It runs on iPhone and Mac and keeps its records as plain files in your own iCloud.

The next payment stays in view on the iPhone widget and in the Mac menu bar. Your AI can read your longbook and update it through longbook's MCP server, and every change it makes shows in Proposals, to keep or undo.

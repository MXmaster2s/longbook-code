# What is longbook Cloud

> longbook Cloud is an optional subscription, $4 a month through Apple, for the features that run on longbook's server. Your longbook folder, both apps and everything in them stay yours without it.

## What it does

### A hosted copy for your AI

Claude on the web or the phone, and ChatGPT, run on someone else's computers, so they cannot open the longbook folder in your iCloud. Cloud keeps a copy of your longbook on longbook's server and serves it at longbook.app/mcp, so those assistants can read it and suggest changes, each one shown in Proposals to keep or undo.

### More statement reading

Every account can have a few statements a month read into charges on longbook's server, with storage off; Cloud raises that to many more. Your own AI can always do the same step.

### Shared reports

A report becomes a link and a password that open it for a few days. Anyone you send it to can read it and leave a comment, and the comments come back into longbook.

## Why it exists

Servers cost money to run, and the folder in your iCloud does not need one. So the apps are a one-time purchase and the part that runs on a server is a monthly subscription. Nothing about your own folder depends on it.

## Questions

### What does it cost, and how do I stop it?

$4 a month, charged by Apple and renewed every month. Buying longbook includes its first 12 months, for everyone in your Apple family too, and Family Sharing covers the subscription after that. Cancel in your Apple ID settings at least 24 hours before the period ends; Apple handles refunds. The terms are at longbook.app/terms.

### What can I do without Cloud?

Everything in the apps. On a Mac, Claude Desktop or Claude Code can read and edit your longbook folder in iCloud, or use the local server, at no charge. A Claude or ChatGPT that runs online, and the iPhone's Claude app, can only reach longbook through the hosted copy, which needs Cloud. A few statements a month are read on the server without it.

### What happens when it ends?

The server features stop. Your folder and the apps are untouched. The hosted copy is deleted 30 days after the subscription ends, and subscribing again brings the connection back.

## About longbook

longbook is a memory for your financial commitments: subscriptions, insurance, rent, loans and every other payment that comes back. It runs on iPhone and Mac and keeps its records as plain files in your own iCloud.

The next payment stays in view on the iPhone widget and in the Mac menu bar. Your AI can read your longbook and update it through longbook's MCP server, and every change it makes shows in Proposals, to keep or undo.

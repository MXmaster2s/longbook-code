A commitment carries one category, and its charges take it. The dashboard, monthly reports and your AI total spending by category.

## On the screen

On iPhone, the **Category** list, opened from the commitment form: a searchable list of categories, from Streaming and Software to Insurance and Rent, the chosen one ticked; a name it does not find is offered to add. On Mac, the **Category** row of the commitment form, one chip per category, under **More Details**.

## What it does

### One category per commitment

longbook comes with categories for subscriptions, streaming, rent, EMIs, insurance, salaries, utilities, education and more, each with its own icon. Any other is added by name. A category is optional.

### Totals by category

The **Categories** card on the dashboard totals the month by category, with each one's share, and each tile opens the list narrowed to that category and month. [Monthly reports](/features/create-monthly-expense-reports/) carry the same breakdown, and **All charges** filters by category.

### One rename, every commitment

**Edit Category** lists every commitment in a category, and a rename updates all of them. It opens with a press and hold on a category in the commitment form, or a right-click on a Mac. A category you added can be deleted; its commitments keep their money and lose the label.

## Why it exists

A total says how much went out, not where. A category on each commitment lets the dashboard, a monthly report and your AI group the same charges the same way, month after month.

## Questions

### Can a charge be in two categories?

No. A commitment carries one category, and every charge takes it. Commitments without one are grouped under **No category**.

### How are imported statement lines categorised?

[Statement import](/features/import-your-bank-statements/) suggests a category for each line, from your categories and longbook's own, or leaves it empty. The review screen changes any of them before the lines are added.

### Can my AI set categories?

Yes. Through [longbook's MCP server](/features/automate-longbook-with-claude/), your AI reads the categories in use and sets one on what it adds or updates. Each change waits in **Proposals**.

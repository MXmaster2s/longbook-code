The next payment in the Mac menu bar: the amount due and how many days away it is, even with every window closed.

## On the screen

The longbook item in the Mac menu bar: the total due on the next payment day and how many days away it is.

## What it does

### The next payment at a glance

The menu bar shows the total due on the next payment day and how far away it is: **Today**, **Tomorrow** or a count of days. Totals are per currency and never converted. Paused and cancelled commitments, charges marked paid, and money in are left out.

### Every payment that day

The menu names each commitment due that day after the total, and lists a transfer, such as a card bill, separately with its own amount. **Open longbook** brings back the window, **Refresh** reads the folder again, and **Quit longbook** closes the app.

### Read from the folder

The menu bar item reads the longbook folder itself, even with every window closed. It reads the folder again when the day changes, when the Mac wakes from sleep, when the app loads a change, and each time the menu opens.

## Why it exists

longbook reminds you of the next payment from the Mac menu bar. It stays there while the app runs, in view until it is marked paid or its day has passed.

## Questions

### Does the menu bar item need a window open?

No. It stays in the menu bar with every longbook window closed, as long as the app is running. To have it there from the moment you log in, turn on **Open at login** in Settings, under **General**, then **Login Items and Menu Bar**. **Quit longbook** removes the item.

### Can longbook live only in the menu bar?

Yes. **Hide the Dock icon**, on the same page, takes longbook out of the Dock and keeps the menu bar item, with **Settings…** in its menu. **Show in the menu bar** turns the item itself on or off.

### Is there a widget for the Mac?

No. On a Mac, the next payment is in the menu bar. The [Home Screen widget](/features/add-longbook-widget-to-your-iphone/) is for iPhone.

### Can the figure be out of date?

Only between reads, and the menu reads the folder again each time it opens. When the folder cannot be read, for example while iCloud is still downloading it, longbook uses the copy it kept when it last loaded the folder, and the line in the menu is marked **from the last copy**.

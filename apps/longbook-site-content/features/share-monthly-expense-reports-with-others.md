A monthly expense report, shared as a password-protected link. Anyone with the link and the password can read it and comment, without a longbook account.

## On the screen

A monthly report on iPhone and Mac, with a Share button under its name. The sharing card at its end holds the link, who it went to and when it expires.

## What it does

### A link and a password

**Share report** makes a link at `longbook.app/r/` and a password of three words and two digits. The moment the link is made, **Report shared** shows the link and the password side by side, each with a copy button, to be sent separately. The password is shown only then; longbook's server keeps only a hash of it.

### Comments on each section

The report opens in a browser once the password is typed, and every section has its own comment box; a name is optional. **Pull comments** writes the comments into the report in your folder, under the section each one is about.

### Three days, or sooner

A link expires 3 days after it is made. **Stop sharing** ends it sooner and deletes the shared copy and its comments from longbook's server. **Shared with** keeps a note of who has the link.

## Why it exists

Household money is often looked at by more than one person: a partner, a parent, an accountant. A link shows one month without handing over the whole longbook, and the questions come back beside the numbers they are about.

## Questions

### What does the person I share with see?

The one report, drawn as the app draws it. For a month longbook drew, that is Total expenses, Accounts, Categories and All charges. No other part of your longbook is sent.

### Do they need longbook?

No. The link opens in a browser and the password unlocks it; reading and commenting need no account. Only the person sharing signs in to longbook.

### What if the password is lost?

longbook cannot show it again. **Stop sharing**, then **Share report** once more for a fresh link and password.

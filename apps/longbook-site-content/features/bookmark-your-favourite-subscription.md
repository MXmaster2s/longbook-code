A bookmark saves a commitment to one short list, kept apart from your records, so the commitments you check most stay together.

## On the screen

**Commitments** on iPhone and Mac: the charges ahead, grouped by month. A swipe right on a row on iPhone, or a right-click on Mac, offers **Mark Paid** and **Bookmark**.

## What it does

### Any commitment

Every commitment offers **Bookmark**: in its row's swipe and menu, and on its details. Once bookmarked, the same place reads **Remove Bookmark**.

### One list

**Bookmarks** opens the saved commitments as one list: from More on iPhone, and from the bookmark icon at the foot of the sidebar on Mac. **Upcoming** shows their charges ahead; **Past** shows the charges made.

### A filter like the others

**Bookmarked Only** in the filters of **Commitments** narrows the list to saved commitments, and combines with the category, account, person, type, status and currency filters.

## Why it exists

A longbook can hold dozens of commitments, and a few are checked more often than the rest: the rent, a card bill, an insurance renewal. A bookmark keeps those together without changing how anything is counted.

## Questions

### Does a bookmark change a commitment?

No. Bookmarks are a display preference, saved in a separate file in the longbook folder, not in your records. Totals, reports and the widget stay the same, and an AI that edits your records leaves bookmarks in place.

### Do bookmarks show on both iPhone and Mac?

Yes, when both open the same longbook folder. The bookmarks file is saved in that folder, beside the records.

### Can my AI see my bookmarks?

No. Claude and other assistants read your commitments through longbook's MCP server, and bookmarks are not part of what it serves. More on [Automate longbook with Claude](/features/automate-longbook-with-claude/).

## 1.4.1

Released 24 October 2026 for iPhone and Mac. This is the first version of longbook on the App Store. Here is what it brings, grouped by what you do with longbook.

### Getting started

- **A clear way in.** Two welcome pages, each with Back, then Sign in with Apple, then Activate your longbook, which leads with the 7-day refund.
- **Buy it, then use it.** An account without a licence opens on the purchase screen, with Restore Purchase and a demo to look around first.
- **12 months of Cloud, and a refund.** A licence includes 12 months of [longbook Cloud](/features/what-is-longbook-cloud/). In Account, Request a Refund is there for 7 days after you buy.
- **Your longbook without iCloud.** If you would rather not use iCloud, longbook can keep your longbook on this iPhone instead. By default it stays in [your own iCloud](/features/keep-your-longbook-in-your-own-icloud/).

### Importing a statement

- **A review that looks like Changes.** Importing a statement shows what was read, with Discard and Add at the top, then the charges, people and repeats one tab at a time. It is the same layout as [Changes](/features/review-every-change-your-ai-makes/).
- **Repeating payments become commitments.** The repeating payments you tick when reading a statement become commitments, planned ahead, so Coming up fills from the first statement.
- **Any currency.** A statement in pounds, dollars or any other currency is read in that currency, not rupees.
- **Kept straight away.** A statement you import is kept at once and filed in History, so Changes does not ask again.
- **Your say before it goes to an AI.** Before longbook first sends a statement to an AI, it asks you to accept the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/), with the policy right there to read. What happens to the statement after that is in [how longbook turns your bank statements into data](/blog/using-ai-for-statements-to-data/).

### Every day

- **Light, Dark or Automatic, at once.** On iPhone, the appearance you pick takes effect straight away, without restarting the app.
- **Search by what the card says.** Search finds commitments by what their cards say: Cancelling, Free trial, Yearly, Paid and more.
- **One kind of list.** Relationship and account type get the same search-or-add list as category.
- **Nothing to review.** When nothing is waiting, Changes says Nothing to review, with a way into History.
- **Large text.** At the largest text sizes, screens rearrange so every word reads in full.

### On the Mac

- **Sheets open in the middle of the window.** Sheets, and Sign in with Apple, open centred in the window.
- **Rows that name no one.** Accounts and People also show the rows that name no one.
- **The timeline reaches further.** Pull the timeline past its end to reveal the button that switches to the next or last 30 payments.

### Your account

- **Deleting says what goes and what stays.** Before your account is deleted, longbook says what goes and what stays, offers to save a copy of your longbook first, and asks you to type DELETE. An account opened with Apple ends its Apple link too.

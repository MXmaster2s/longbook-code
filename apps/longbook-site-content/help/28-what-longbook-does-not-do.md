# What longbook does not do

## It doesn't

- **connect to your bank.** Payments are typed in, read from a statement you pick, or added by your AI or Siri.
- **cancel a subscription.** It records the cancellation. The cancelling happens with the provider.
- **set budgets, targets or forecasts.** It records and totals what goes out.
- **convert currencies.** Each currency is totalled on its own.
- **write to Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.** The calendar is drawn inside longbook.
- **read your email itself.** Your AI reads it through its own connector.
- **keep your statements.** They are read and not kept. A copy of your longbook goes to our server only while **Let AI Read My longbook** is on.
- **offer a Lock Screen widget** or a Mac widget. The iPhone has a Home Screen widget and the Mac has a menu bar item.

## Coming later

- more devices: iPad, Apple Watch, iPhone Duo and Android, with the same licence
- **Income**: salary, interest and refunds in their own list
- **Available to spend**: what is left after everything already committed
- **Savings accounts**: your saved balance kept apart from spending

None of these is in the app yet.

# What each screen in longbook is for

## On iPhone

The bottom bar has five places:

- **Dashboard**: the next payment, the month's spending and where it went.
- **Calendar**: every payment on its day.
- **Add**: a new commitment or expense.
- **Reports**: one report for every month that has a confirmed charge.
- **Settings**: everything else.

**Search** is the first row in Settings. It finds anything in your longbook, and before you type it shows your bookmarks and your recent searches.

Settings opens everything else: **Search**, **Proposals**, **Insights**, **Bookmarks**, **Accounts**, **People**, **Import a statement** and **Add accounts and people found in statements**, **Trips**, **AI Connections**, **Display & currency**, **Data & privacy**, **Optimize your data**, **Try the demo**, **Tips**, **Tutorials**, **Help & about**, **What’s new**, and your account at the top.

## On Mac

The sidebar lists them all, in one list:

- At the top: **Search**. Press ⌘K from any screen, type, and the results drop down grouped by kind. ↑ and ↓ move, Return opens the highlighted one on its own screen, and Escape closes the results.
- Dashboard, Reports, Calendar, Commitments, Charges, Earnings, Transfers, Insights and Proposals.
- **Accounts**, with your starred accounts a little inside it.
- **People**, with your starred people a little inside it.
- At the bottom: the light and dark switch and the bookmark icon. **Settings…** (⌘,), **What’s New**, **Check for Updates…** and **Quit longbook** are in the longbook menu, and **Ask Support…** is in the **Help** menu.

Star an account or a person on its screen to keep it in the sidebar. A click on a starred row opens it on that screen.

A list comes back as you left it: the row you last opened is open again, and on **Accounts** and **People** your column widths are kept, one set with the side panel open and one with it closed. Close the panel with its × and the screen opens with nothing chosen next time.

## What each one is

- **Commitments**: every payment ahead, grouped by month. **Past** lists every charge made.
- **Proposals**: what your AI or Siri wrote, to keep or discard. Its **History** also holds each statement import.
- **Insights**: short notes your AI left on your cards.
- **Accounts**: your banks, cards and wallets, each with its balance.
- **People**: who each payment is for.
- **Bookmarks**: everything you bookmarked, from every screen, in one list.
- **Search**: accounts, commitments, charges, earnings, transfers, people, proposals and help articles, by name, account or person, category, note, statement line or amount. It searches on your device only.

## Settings

On Mac, Settings is a window of its own, like the Mac's System Settings. Its sidebar has a search field, your longbook account, then **General**, **Appearance**, **AI**, **Statements**, **Data & Privacy** and **Tutorials**. Back and Forward (⌘[ and ⌘]) move between pages. **AI** is where you connect your AI, copy prompts, and set up Siri and Shortcuts; on iPhone that's **AI Connections**.

**Appearance** also holds **Show decimals**, off by default, which adds the cents to amounts on screen; your records always keep the exact amount. **Card colours** sets the colour of each kind of card, from 20 colours, one colour to a kind.

## Questions

### Where do I add a payment?
Tap **Add** on iPhone. On Mac, click **Add Commitment** on **Commitments** or **Calendar**, or choose **File › New Commitment** (⌘N) from any screen.

### How do I find something quickly?
Use **Search**: ⌘K or the field at the top of the sidebar on Mac, or the first row in Settings on iPhone. **Show all** under a kind opens that screen with the same words in its search field.

### Where do I see what my AI changed?
In **Proposals**.
